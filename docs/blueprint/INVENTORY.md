## Observations for Navigation and Route Planning

1. **Trailing slashes 404.** The Worker explicitly handles `/about`, `/about/standards`, `/about/diana`, `/development`, and `/development/atlas`. Adding new nested routes (e.g., `/reading-room/essays`) will fail unless the Worker is updated. **All new routes should be flat pages** (e.g., `/reading-room`) or query-based (e.g., `/ether?mood=noir-jazz`), not new nested paths.

2. **Generated pages:** `src/publication-pages.mjs` creates 16 pages dynamically from data. Any route changes for those pages require updating the `pages` object in that file.

3. **Existing content must be preserved.** All legal pages (privacy, standards), learning paths, foundation concepts, project ledger, and the 18 published essays remain accessible. When department names or routes change, provide redirects or discoverable navigation links.

4. **Music and Ether Room placement:** Music is currently at `/music` with the Ether Room at `/ether`. The #60 map makes Music a top-level department housing the Ether Room, not a subsection of Culture. Existing `/ether` and `/music` routes must be preserved; navigation should reflect the new structure.

5. **Sample and concept material:** The Foundation, Pacific Royal Academy, and several projects are explicitly labeled as concepts, not operating institutions. All labels and disclaimers must remain accurate.
