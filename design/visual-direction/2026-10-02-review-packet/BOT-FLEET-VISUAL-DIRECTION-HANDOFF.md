## House rule

> SAVRONO is cinematic, material, intelligent, and composed. The publisher controls evidence and reader trust; the desks control atmosphere, pacing, image grammar, and invitation.

### Shared controls, never optional

- evidence, attribution, rights, accessibility, corrections, disclosure, and reader agency;
- original creator/publisher identified for outward references;
- generated art labeled where a reasonable reader could mistake it for reportage;
- no fake contributors, documentary claims, real-time operational claims, endorsements, or copied third-party identity;
- no automatic runtime work from documentation guidance.

### Do not flatten the desks

- Do **not** create one uniform dark dashboard and recolor it nine times.
- Do **not** treat every desk as a universal funnel.
- Do **not** make direct cross-desk travel compulsory. Invitations are allowed; forced integration is not.
- Do **not** turn Tech Lounge into generic blue SaaS or cyberpunk spectacle.
- Do **not** treat the Lifestyle working name as final.

## Approved design direction: Tech Lounge / Afterimage Workshop

The live `/tech-lounge` route is a **preservation baseline**, not missing work. It already provides a warm, after-hours technology salon: tangible computing, soft amber/violet light, editorial typography, visual reading, and archive media.

Future Tech direction merges that warmth with an internal precision layer:

| Layer | Required direction |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival | Ink-black / aubergine room; tangible machine or workbench; one statement image; not a dashboard. |
| Information | Hard modular grid; mono labels only for provenance and state; source cards; clear *reported / essay / archive / speculative / in development* labeling. |
| Palette | Obsidian, smoky aubergine, warm ivory, signal amber, restrained circuit cyan; cyan never becomes the whole page. |
| Image grammar | Original macro hardware, workbenches, archival-computing texture, nocturnal infrastructure, clear provenance. |
| Motion/sound | Discrete only; reduced-motion safe; sound opt-in; never essential for comprehension. |
| Human figure | Optional atmosphere only; never a factual technical authority or security-event dramatization. |
