# SAVRONO Visual Direction Catalogue

## Governing idea

> **SAVRONO is cinematic, material, intelligent, and composed. The publisher enforces reader trust; each desk creates its own atmosphere, pace, image grammar, and invitation.**

This is a direction system, not a page template. A reader may remain entirely inside one desk. Cross-desk links are editorial invitations, not a universal funnel.

## House constants

| Constant | Practical effect |
| --- | --- |
| Editorial cinema | Images establish a claim, threshold, or texture rather than decorate a card. Use deliberate crop, light, and negative space. |
| Material intelligence | Machines, paper, garments, food, archival records, instruments, and landscapes have surface, provenance, and consequence. |
| Warm/cold tension | Amber, paper, dusk, cyan, graphite, violet, or signal green may coexist. Accents belong to desks, not one global palette. |
| Readable rigor | Large editorial display type sits with compact metadata, source labels, captions, and accessible contrast. |
| Evidence before atmosphere | Reporting and research distinguish source, archive, essay, art, opinion, speculative material, and in-development work. |
| Reader agency | Outbound references identify original publisher/creator and editorial reason. No disguised aggregation, copied work, or implied affiliation. |

## Publisher controls versus desk freedom

| Layer | Non-negotiable | Intentionally variable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence and rights | Attribution, provenance, disclosure, corrections, rights review, accessibility. | Editorial medium, visual reference, curation rhythm. |
| Navigation | Reader escape routes and accessible controls. | Arrival sequence and optional paths to another desk. |
| Typography | Readability, hierarchy, legible metadata. | Serif/sans/mono balance, display scale, density. |
| Image handling | Caption / status honesty and no misleading documentary claim. | Photo, illustration, archive, macro, object study, conceptual generation. |
| Motion and sound | Optional, reduced-motion safe, never required for comprehension. | Pace, silence, rhythm, visual transition language. |

## Desk direction map

| Desk | Sensory premise | Information behavior | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover Story | Quarterly centerpiece, singular premise, bespoke visual production. | Clear distinction between verified reporting, essay, and companion media. | Treating a cover image as a pre-approved story or production asset. |
| The Daily Desk | Evidence field, alert but restrained. | Date, source, status, corrections, and outward references are visible. | Crisis spectacle, faux classified aesthetics, invented reporting. |
| Lifestyle *(working)* | Material life: culture, food, travel, fashion/design, visual botanical culture. | Cultural context and creator/source clarity. | Recipe/medical instruction, cannabis promotion, luxury-ad template. |
| MOTOR | Engineering, play, surface, and velocity across machines and motion. | Machine/source specifics only when verified. | Automotive-only scope, brand endorsement, unverified specifications. |
| Music | Listening room, live texture, human timing. | Original artists/publishers named; playback/links remain opt-in. | Generic album-card grid and auto-play. |
| Academic Journal | Quiet, rigorous, long-form. | Method, author, citation, and uncertainty are visible. | Faux peer review or invented institutional credential. |
| Tech Lounge | After-hours workbench: objects, systems, labor, culture, consequence. | Clear status labels: reported, essay, archive, speculative, in development. | Generic blue dashboard, cyberpunk spectacle, security theater. |
| In Development | Studio threshold and honest incompleteness. | Clearly mark concept, prototype, paused, or not public. | Implying availability or a committed release. |
| About SAVRONO | Publisher orientation and reader rights. | Mission, standards, corrections, disclosure, contact/escape paths. | Treating Infinity Enterprises and SAVRONO as interchangeable. |

## Visual studies as review evidence

The six studies in [`studies/`](studies/) are **original generated conceptual art**. They test a direction; they do not establish routes, content, facts, public claims, or final art. Generated visual material must be disclosed if a reasonable reader could mistake it for reporting photography.

## Future implementation rule

No runtime use follows from this catalogue alone. A later Ready contract must cite current architecture, this or a successor desk bible, source and asset baseline, provenance/disclosure status, exact files, focused tests, desktop/phone evidence, reviewer, and target branch.
