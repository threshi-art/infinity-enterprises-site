## Asset metadata required before production use

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Creator / owner / license or release | Establishes authority to use the asset. |
| Date, location, subject, and caption | Preserves context and reduces misleading use. |
| Alteration and generation status | Distinguishes photography, archive, illustration, and generation. |
| Desk, story, and placement | Prevents a generic asset library from silently becoming public IA. |
| Required disclosure and credit | Preserves reader agency and rights compliance. |
| Review and approval record | Makes a later use auditable. |

## File integrity
