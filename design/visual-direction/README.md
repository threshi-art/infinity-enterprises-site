# SAVRONO visual-direction concept reference

**Status:** owner-selected atmosphere reference; design study only.

**Related:** #125, #76, #109, #107, and historical PR #77.

## What this reference is

This directory preserves one generated concept board that the owner selected on 2026-10-02 because it captures the intended **SAVRONO vibe**: cinematic editorial scale, dark orientation fields, warm reading surfaces and accents, strong serif display hierarchy, distinctive desk worlds, generous imagery, and a publication experience that can feel premium on both desktop and phone.

The board is **generated conceptual art** created during a principal-architect-led design exploration. It is not a screenshot of the current site, not a live-system image, not reportage, and not evidence that the depicted pages or assets have been implemented.

The owner also explicitly wants to preserve and build from the current Infinity Enterprises site and the existing Wiki brand/marketing work. This reference therefore **adds visual-direction evidence; it does not replace existing work**.

## What is illustrative, not authoritative

The concept board contains embedded sample copy for visual-composition purposes. The following are illustrative only:

- the displayed issue date;
- sample headlines and story copy;
- the exact navigation labels and count;
- the exact masthead/wordmark treatment;
- the depicted photography and individual art selections;
- subscription calls to action;
- the simplified site map;
- any implication that a depicted desk or page is already implemented.

For current architecture and product authority, use #107 and the SAVRONO Publication Architecture. SAVRONO remains a working name pending clearance, Lifestyle remains a working label, and the former fixed-count / fourteen-department / literal-ten-world maps remain historical reference only.

## Preservation baseline

Any future implementation influenced by this board must begin with the actual existing experience, not overwrite it by default. Preserve and compare against:

- the current public home and its editorial work;
- the current MOTOR treatment and its broad future machines-and-motion scope;
- Ether Room slot 01 and the existing music/atmosphere work;
- FORM and other immersive-room identities;
- existing articles, routes, artwork, assets, learning paths, foundation concepts, and project history;
- the existing Wiki Brand Positioning and Marketing and Federated Editorial Worlds direction.

The durable design principle is **federation, not flattening**: shared publisher standards with desk-specific lighting, pace, typography, image grammar, motion and atmosphere.

## What may be borrowed

Reviewers may use this board to discuss:

- dark cinematic shell and strong first-viewport composition;
- warm ivory / copper or gold-like accents as editorial contrast;
- expressive serif display type paired with restrained sans-serif navigation;
- major Cover Story scale and hierarchy;
- asymmetric editorial sequencing rather than identical card grids;
- MOTOR as a visually powerful machines-and-motion world;
- Lifestyle as a warmer, tactile editorial world while the label remains provisional;
- Ether as a distinctly atmospheric, sound-aware world;
- coherent desktop/mobile editorial identity without making every desk look the same.

These are **directional qualities**, not component specifications.

## What must not be copied blindly

Do not infer approval for the concept board's exact copy, date, routes, navigation count, photography, masthead, subscription model, final Lifestyle name, or production art.

Do not use this file as a runtime/public-site asset without a separate asset/content contract, provenance review, accessibility evidence, a focused Ready handoff, implementation PR, independent exact-head review, and separate release/Sites authority.
