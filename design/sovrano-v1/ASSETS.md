# SOVRANO Infinitum: Ember's art assets (manifest)

Everything here was made by Ember on 2026-09-26/27. Nothing is hand-drawn or photographed.

## Where each asset should be used
- `masthead/masthead-wordmark.png`: homepage masthead, ivory SOVRANO over champagne INFINITUM, transparent, 1346x364. Use it on the navy cover.
- `masthead/masthead-crest-lockup.png`: crest plus wordmark, transparent, 1822x469. Use it in the header bar or footer.
- `sovrano-crest/crest-*.png` (1600x2000), `badge-*.png` (1000x1000), `favicon-{16,32,180,512}.png`. Crest year MMXXVI is a PLACEHOLDER until the founding year is confirmed.
- `editorial-art/01-04*.png` (1024x576): Home portrait, lounge, Motore GT, House of SOVRANO study plate. Upscale before full-bleed use.
- `editorial-art/covers/cover-<room>.png` (1280x720): section covers for daily-desk, mercati, moda, viaggi, tavola, sport, tech and reading-room. One third of each frame is left empty for the title.
- `logo-drafts/`: earlier wordmark explorations, for reference only.
