The Infinity

Proposed nonprofit initiative / In development
The charter / In formation

Knowledge with
a public purpose.

The Infinity Foundation is being designed as a charitable organization for research and education across law, artificial intelligence, and civilization studies. Its proposed youth initiatives would connect care, opportunity, and rigorous learning.

We intend a 501(c)(3) charitable structure. This page describes a plan; it does not represent that the foundation has received an IRS determination, opened programs, or begun accepting donations.

Current status: Concept and program design. Legal formation, governance, recognition, funding, and operating approvals remain to be established.
