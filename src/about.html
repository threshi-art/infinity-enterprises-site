The enterprise / What connects the work

One house.
Many rooms.

Infinity Enterprises brings together original software, governed AI, research notes, civic proposals, and editorial work. This publication is the public reading room for that portfolio. Project Atlas records the work and its stage; the Research Journal follows methods and questions; Agentic@Enigmas holds essays and interpretation.

The Infinity Foundation and Pacific Royal Academy are proposed initiatives. Their formation, funding, permissions, and operations are still to be established. We label concepts and prototypes as such.

  • Editorial work: essays, research notes, and technology culture.
  • Products: software and AI systems at distinct stages of development.
  • Public purpose: civic and educational concepts that need independent testing.
