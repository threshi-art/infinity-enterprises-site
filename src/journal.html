Journal watch / September reading

From the
literature.

Three published papers that sharpen the cover question. These links go to the publishers and proceedings; the summaries are editorial reading notes, not claims that Infinity produced the research.

01

What large language models know and what people think they know

Steyvers and colleagues · Nature Machine Intelligence · 2025

Participants could become more confident in an answer when the model explained it at greater length, even when the extra words did not improve accuracy. A design prompt for the cover: how should a system communicate uncertainty without mistaking fluency for proof?

Read the paper ↗
02

On the Quest for Effectiveness in Human Oversight

Sterz and colleagues · ACM FAccT · 2024

An interdisciplinary account asks what makes oversight meaningful in practice. A human review button alone does not settle questions of authority, understanding, or the ability to intervene.

Read the paper ↗
03

Detecting hallucinations using semantic entropy

Farquhar and colleagues · Nature · 2024

The authors study uncertainty across the meanings of possible answers as a way to flag confabulations. It is one method to examine, not a blanket guarantee that an answer is reliable.

Editorial standard

Show the work.

This journal distinguishes a working note from a tested result and a published study. Future entries can carry their question, method, sources, limits, and next check.

  • Identify whether an entry is original research, an academic exercise, or a design proposal.
  • Link source material only after authorship, privacy, and citation review.
  • Update a claim when new evidence changes it.
