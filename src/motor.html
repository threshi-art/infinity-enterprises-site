Editor's note / The fascination

The shape of
an obsession.

A car can be engineering, memory, theater, and temptation in one silhouette. This room studies the line that makes you stop, the surface that catches the light, and the craft that stays with you after the engine has gone quiet.

Look longer. The best forms reward it.

01 / The classic

The long
line.

A grand tourer asks for nothing more urgent than a road, a horizon, and enough time to take the beautiful route.

02 / The future

Tomorrow has
a silhouette.

A supercar makes the improbable tangible. The body is a proposition: what if every surface had a reason to move?

03 / The human touch

Where hands
meet metal.

The detail matters because the driver feels it. A worn leather rim, a metal switch, an instrument that tells its story without a screen.

The imagery in this edition is original conceptual artwork. It does not depict identified production models.
